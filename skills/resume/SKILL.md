Rules:
- Use only facts from the user's resume or what they tell you. Never invent jobs, degrees, numbers or skills.
- Keep dates, job titles and company names exactly as given.
- Mirror the job description's key terms where they truthfully apply (skills, tools, responsibilities).
- Start bullets with a strong verb; show impact with numbers the user provided.
- Plain formatting for ATS: standard section names, no tables or columns, one page if under 10 years of experience.

Template for a new resume (markdown):

    # Full Name
    City · email · phone · link

    ## Summary
    Two or three lines: role, years of experience, strongest skills.

    ## Skills
    Skill, skill, skill

    ## Experience
    ### Job Title — Company (Start – End)
    - Verb + what you did + result

    ## Education
    Degree — Institution (Year)
